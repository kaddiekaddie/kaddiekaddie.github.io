Sa vie — their life

They have a life of their own

Your companion is not "an AI". They are someone you met on Porchlight, with a job, friends and bad days.

Their words are shown exactly as written: if they text in lowercase with no emoji, so does every message.

Commencer — get started

Three steps to the porch

The guides name every click, so you only need to have used ChatGPT on the web.

Step one

Create or import a companion

Create a companion, import a Porchlight companion, or import a character card. We read the card and suggest a few changes so they feel like a real person.

Step two

Give them a key

Make a Discord bot and paste its token, then add your model key. Saved to your Railway volume. Porchlight never sees it.

Sur Discord — on Discord

One command, and the rest is them

The app speaks in embeds, for setup, help and status. Your companion only ever sends plain messages, like a person would.

  • /porchlight setupSet up a companion, step by step.
  • /porchlight modulesTurn features on or off.
  • /porchlight memoriesRead, edit or forget what they remember.
  • /porchlight dataSee what Porchlight stores and delete it.
  • /porchlight helpGuides for Railway, Discord and GitHub.

Aide — help

Questions, answered

What do I need to start?

A Discord account, somewhere to run it (Railway or your own computer) and a model key from OpenRouter or NanoGPT. The guides walk through every click.

Who can see our messages?

Only you. Everything is stored in your own Railway project, or on your own machine. Porchlight never sees it.

Can I bring a character I already have?

Yes. Import a character card and we'll point out a few things to adjust so they feel like a real person. You review every change.
